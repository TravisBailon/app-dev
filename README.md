# 🎬 My Favorite Movies and Series

## 😂 Comedy
### **Brooklyn 99**
> “Cool, cool, cool, cool, cool — no doubt, no doubt.”  
A hilarious police sitcom that follows the detectives of the **99th precinct** in Brooklyn.  
It’s full of *wit, teamwork, and unforgettable characters* like **Jake Peralta** and **Captain Holt**.

### **Grown Ups**
A fun movie about childhood friends who reunite as adults.  
It reminds me that **growing up doesn’t mean losing your sense of fun**.  

## 🕵️‍♂️ Action & Thriller
### **The Blacklist**
This series follows **Raymond "Red" Reddington**, a criminal mastermind who helps the FBI catch dangerous fugitives.  

## 💥 Anime & Drama
- **One Punch Man**  
  A superhero who defeats any enemy with a *single punch* 💥.
